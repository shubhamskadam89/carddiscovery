Why it helps. The server can sync every day even when the phone is off, and a stolen phone never exposes Gmail access.

Quota. A project-wide meter watches the 80M units/day limit, alerts at 60% and 80%, and above 90% delays the slow background jobs while new users still get their quick scan.

Let search classify

  • has:attachment filename:pdf proves a statement PDF exists, with no read
  • -category:promotions and offer words drop ads before we pay for them
  • Exact sender address per email type: statements, alerts and offers come from different addresses

Know which layer each template needs

  • Record per bank template where the last 4 and product appear: subject, preview or body
  • Never pay 60 units when 20 is enough
  • Rule knowledge, not user data, so it is shared across all users

Stop early, never pay twice

  • A card is confirmed and no other numbers show at that bank: stop reading that bank
  • Thread IDs, free in search results, skip replies and duplicates
  • The evidence table skips anything already read

Cheaper PDFs

  • Only the newest statement per card: limit, points and due in one file
  • Skip the PDF when the statement email already gives the total and points
  • Older PDFs only if a feature needs history

What stays, what never does, and leaving

Kept in Postgres

  • Cards: bank, product, network, last 4 (what RBI allows)
  • Evidence per email: type, date, flags, no text
  • Statement totals: limit, points, fees, due
  • Encrypted Gmail token and your Yes/No answers

Never stored

  • Email text, HTML or PDFs (worker memory only)
  • Full card numbers (blanked on sight)
  • OTP codes or Google passwords in plain text
  • Anything for ads, lending or model training

Disconnect

Revokeat Google
